Built for game night

Less bookkeeping.
More playing.

Arcana Table helps your group keep shared game state visible and organized without trying to replace the rules or the cards.

♙

One view per player

Check life totals, turns, commander status, hand and library counts, and public zones at a glance.

⌁

Play together nearby

Connect Android devices over local Wi-Fi and keep the table, turn, readiness, and chat in sync.

▤

Bring your deck

Import a deck list, review unmatched cards, and edit the cards you’re tracking across game zones.

◷

Keep the turn moving

Track turn phases, draw cards, pass turns, and use quick access to the stack and game log.

✧

Useful automation

Supported life, draw, and token effects can resolve automatically, with notices and manual prompts when needed.

↕

Make the board yours

Resize the hand panel, inspect card details, move cards between zones, and keep the player view compact.
