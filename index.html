Privacy & security

Safer rooms for casual play.

Internet rooms use secure WebSockets, require a room code and password, and filter hidden cards from what other players receive.

01 / CONNECTION

Encrypted connection

The Internet client requires WSS, which protects room traffic while it travels between devices and the room service.

02 / HIDDEN CARDS

Private hands and libraries

Each player receives a filtered game view. Other players’ libraries and unrevealed hands are withheld.

03 / PLAYER SEATS

Basic seat permissions

The room service assigns player seats and limits which seat can update player data and shared turn or stack state.

Prototype status: Players share a room password, and the service does not enforce all Magic rules or provide full anti-cheat protection. Use a long, unique room password and keep sensitive information out of chat. Internet rooms also require the room service to be deployed separately.
