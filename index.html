Before you join

What’s available in the current test.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Offline games against local AI run on your device.

Does Build 141 support four players?

No. Build 141 is focused on two-player games. Four-player support is still in development.

Can I import a deck?

No. Public deck import is turned off for the current test. Premade Commander decks are available.

Where can I get Build 141?

Build 141 is a debug test APK and has not been uploaded to Google Play or released publicly. The closed-test form requests a Google Play invitation; it does not distribute this APK.
