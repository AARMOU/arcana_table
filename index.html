Build 138 update

A lot has changed since Build 121.

A more capable Commander table, with integrated multiplayer, deck tools, a full tutorial, and targeted usability repairs. These features are present in the Build 138 candidate; physical multiplayer acceptance is ongoing.

01 · Multiplayer preview

Start at the table, not in setup pop-ups.

Host directly into a four-seat friends lobby, adjust seats before starting, share an online room code or Wi-Fi address, and have guests ready up. Two-to-four-seat room flows are in active testing.

02 · Stay connected

Room chat and private hands.

In-room chat has returned for Wi-Fi and internet sessions. Opening-hand and mulligan flows use guest-private card data, and a background-host guard helps keep an active room alive. Reconnect and device testing remain important.

03 · Your decks

Premades, import, edit.

Browse categorized decks from Arcana, Wizards precon content, and Argentum selections. The deck import entry and deck editor are enabled in this test candidate. Card legality and individual interactions remain subject to engine support.

04 · Learning the table

20 chapters of guided play.

Follow interactive lessons through the opening hand, battlefield, combat, stack and controls. Lessons include real Argentum-powered practice states, with improved guidance placement for smaller and folding screens.

05 · More readable play

Refined table and menus.

Touch-friendly hand, stack, turn and commander views; adjustable layouts and notifications; cleaner menu flows. The obsolete separate “Online with Argentum” menu entry has been removed, leaving direct Host and Join actions.

06 · Release polish

Original identity, useful diagnostics.

The original Arcana launcher logo is retained. The optional in-game “engine running” indicator lives under Help & Diagnostics; the required Android foreground-service notice is separate. Release assets and branding are checked during CI.

07 · Cards and rules

Growing Argentum coverage.

More custom-card behaviors, Eldrazi work, and engine regressions are covered by automated tests. The original Eldrazi Incursion index reached 94 of 100 card-name slots in the build evidence; indexed does not mean fully gameplay-certified.

08 · Build quality

Release candidate tested.

The Build 138 production-package job completed with 427 Android tests, zero failures and five skips, plus custom-card, engine, release-lint and branding checks. Passing CI does not replace real-device playtesting.

Release status — October 27, 2026

The Build 138 Android App Bundle has been compiled and signed for the existing com.arcanatable Play listing. Google Play acceptance or closed-testing rollout has not been confirmed. Tester access remains invitation-based.

Development milestones

From Build 121 to Build 138.

Major player-facing workstreams, rather than a claim that every individual bug or card has been fully resolved.

  • Builds 121–131 · Engine and play foundations

    Argentum integration, additional card scenarios and regression coverage, targeting and combat investigations, improvements to hand/table usability, and expanded Eldrazi testing. Some complex game interactions remain active issues.

  • Builds 132–136 · Release and multiplayer groundwork

    Original launcher branding, Help & Diagnostics cleanup, the interactive tutorial, local and internet room infrastructure, multi-seat host and guest flows, and the new full-screen waiting lobby.

  • Builds 137–138 · Integrated test candidate

    Four-seat friends lobby without redundant host setup, guest-private opening hands, in-room chat, background-host improvements, Fold tutorial placement, and simplified online menu. Build 138 additionally passed production-ID APK/AAB validation and signing.

See development coordination and release evidence, Build 138 CI results, and source code. Source tests, candidate availability, and live Play Store status are different milestones.

Before you join

What to expect in testing.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Offline games against local AI run on your device.

Does Build 138 support four friends?

The four-seat lobby and local/internet room paths are implemented as a testing preview, not a guarantee of finished cross-network reliability. Testers are helping verify real devices and reconnection.

Are deck importing and editing available?

Both are enabled in the Build 138 candidate. Premade decks are still a quick way to get started; individual imported cards depend on current Argentum rules support.

Are the development test scenarios included?

No. Dedicated debugging boards are excluded from the Play release. Interactive tutorial lessons and their practice exercises are included.
